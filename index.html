How It Works

THE CREDIT
SYSTEM.

1
⚡
Assign Credits
Give each app a daily time budget. Instagram 30 mins. YouTube 45 mins. You decide what each app is worth.
2
⏳
Credits Drain
Every second you spend on an app costs a credit. Watch the bar drop in real time. Feel the cost of your attention.
3
🔒
Hard Block
When credits hit zero — the app is blocked. Full-screen lock. No bypass. No "ignore for 15 minutes." Done.

BUILT TO
ENFORCE.
